# AI Usage Log

This document tracks how AI tools are used during the research and writing process for the RingoStrike paper.

## AI Tools Used For

* Brainstorming research angles
* Structuring the paper
* Creating outlines
* Improving clarity and academic tone
* Summarizing research directions
* Creating draft tables and frameworks
* Planning user study materials
* Reviewing claims and identifying missing evidence

## AI Tools Not Used For

* Fabricating citations
* Inventing user data
* Creating fake study results
* Making unsupported scientific claims
* Replacing author judgment
* Claiming validation that has not happened

## Responsibility Statement

The author is responsible for verifying all sources, checking claims, reviewing AI-assisted text, and ensuring that the final paper accurately represents the RingoStrike project and its current level of evidence.

## Current Status

AI is being used as a research, writing, planning, and editing assistant. All claims, sources, and final interpretations must be reviewed and approved by the author.
