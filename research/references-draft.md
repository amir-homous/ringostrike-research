### Citation consistency problems

* The paper uses narrative citation forms such as “Michie, van Stralen, and West (2011).” In strict APA 7 style, sources with three or more authors are generally cited as “Michie et al. (2011)” from the first citation. This should be standardized during final editing.
* The paper uses both American and British spelling in source titles and discussion, including “behavior,” “behaviour,” “behavior change,” and “Behaviour Change Wheel.” Source titles should preserve the original spelling, but the paper’s own prose should choose one style unless quoting or naming a formal framework.
* The current paper has in-text author-year mentions but no complete References section yet. Once this file is added, every in-text citation should be checked against the reference list one by one.
* The reference entries above are based on the metadata currently available in the repository files. They should not be treated as verified publication metadata.

## Important Warning

This References draft must be manually checked before publication, preprint upload, academic submission, portfolio publication, or public sharing.

Before the final paper is submitted, verify every source against the original publication record. At minimum, check:

* author names and author order
* publication year
* article or paper title
* journal or conference title
* volume, issue, and page numbers
* article number, if used
* DOI
* URL format
* capitalization rules required by the target venue
* whether the source is actually cited in the final paper text

No source should be included in the final paper unless it has been verified. A reference that looks correct but has not been checked can still damage the credibility of the paper.

AI-assisted drafting can help organize references, but it must not replace author verification. The author is responsible for checking all sources, claims, citations, and final interpretations before publication.
